This study used publicly available datasets from the Gene Expression Omnibus: GSE268807 (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE268807), GSE140393 (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE140393) and GSE190452 (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE190452). GSE268807 is the discovery dataset; GSE140393 and GSE190452 provide contextual evidence only. Processed summary tables, figure-generation input files and the materials needed to reproduce the results are available in the accompanying GitHub repository at https://github.com/vermouth-SVG/fcdii-astrocyte-regulon-perturbation. Large raw single-cell matrices, large intermediate objects, cisTarget ranking databases, motif annotation files and other third-party resources are not redistributed and should be obtained from the original providers under their respective access terms and licenses.
