This study used publicly available datasets from the Gene Expression Omnibus, including GSE268807, GSE140393 and GSE190452. Fang 2025 / HRA010445 was used only as published contextual evidence; controlled-access raw matrices from that source were not incorporated into the present analysis. Processed summary tables, figure-generation inputs and reproducibility materials supporting this manuscript are available in the accompanying GitHub repository: https://github.com/vermouth-SVG/fcdii-astrocyte-regulon-perturbation. Large raw single-cell matrices, large intermediate objects, cisTarget ranking databases, motif annotation files and other third-party resources are not redistributed and should be obtained from the original data repositories or database providers according to their respective access terms and licenses.
