This study used publicly available single-cell datasets from the Gene Expression Omnibus, including GSE268807, GSE140393, and GSE190452. Fang 2025 / HRA010445 was used only as published pathway-level contextual evidence; controlled-access raw matrices from that source were not incorporated into the present analysis. The accompanying GitHub repository at https://github.com/vermouth-SVG/fcdii-astrocyte-regulon-perturbation provides analysis scripts, processed summary tables, figure-generation inputs and outputs, and reproducibility notes supporting the manuscript. Large raw single-cell matrices, derived large intermediate objects, Docker image layers, and large third-party resources such as cisTarget ranking databases and motif annotation files are not hosted in the GitHub repository. These files should be obtained from GEO, AertsLab/cisTarget, or the relevant original data providers according to their respective access terms and licenses.
