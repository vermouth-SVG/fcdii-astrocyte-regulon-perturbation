Figure3_celloracle_perturbation
==============================

Caption title:
Fig. 3. In silico perturbation prioritizes NFE2L2 and THRB as complementary regulatory anchors

Revision scope:
- Main-text Fig. 3 v3.1 is a small pre-submission visual revision of v3.
- The scientific structure and input data are unchanged from v3.
- Robustness, supportive external/contextual evidence, exploratory drug-signature clues, and pySCENIC regulon landscape panels were not included.
- No complete figure suptitle is drawn inside the graphic; panel titles only.

Input files:
- Metric table: celloracle_run/ko_round1/round1_ko_master_table.csv
- NFE2L2 vector field scores: celloracle_run/ko_round1/NFE2L2/NFE2L2_state_shift_scores.csv
- THRB vector field scores: celloracle_run/ko_round1/THRB/THRB_state_shift_scores.csv

Metric columns used:
- TF: tf
- Overall shift / mean shift length: mean_shift_length
- Approx. Recovery Index: recovery_index
- Mean net shift: mean_net_shift
- Lesion shift: lesion_mean_shift_length
- Control shift: internal_control_mean_shift_length

    tf,overall_shift,approx_ri,net_shift,lesion_shift,control_shift

Panel A encoding:
- x-axis = Mean shift length.
- y-axis = Approx. Recovery Index.
- point size = Mean net shift when available; otherwise uniform size.
- NFE2L2 is highlighted as the highest mean shift, and THRB as the highest Approx. RI.

Panel B scorecard:
- Rows: NFE2L2, THRB, BHLHE40, SOX2.
- Metrics: Overall shift, Approx. RI, Net shift, Lesion shift, Int-control shift.
- Each metric column uses min-max normalization within the four TFs for horizontal mini-bar length; numeric values are printed beside bars.
- Interpretation labels were shortened for v3.1: NFE2L2 = dominant shift; THRB = recovery anchor; BHLHE40 = secondary; SOX2 = retained.
- Control shift refers to internal-control-specific mean shift.

Panel C/D vector field sources:
- Simulated KO shift columns are embedding_x/embedding_y and delta_x/delta_y from each TF state_shift_scores.csv.
- Very light grey background cell points were added in v3.1 using the same embedding_x/embedding_y arrow start coordinates from each state_shift_scores.csv file.
- Randomized-control columns were not plotted in the main figure.
- Vector column mapping: {'NFE2L2': {'x': 'embedding_x', 'y': 'embedding_y', 'dx': 'delta_x', 'dy': 'delta_y', 'group': 'group', 'shift_length': 'shift_length'}, 'THRB': {'x': 'embedding_x', 'y': 'embedding_y', 'dx': 'delta_x', 'dy': 'delta_y', 'group': 'group', 'shift_length': 'shift_length'}}

Arrow downsampling:
- Background points include all available cells in each TF vector table.
- Arrows are downsampled with a fixed random seed (7) to at most 800 arrows per panel and were made slightly darker/wider in v3.1.
- Final arrow counts: {'NFE2L2': 800, 'THRB': 800}

Required interpretation notes:
Figure 3 v3.1 uses existing CellOracle outputs only; no CellOracle or pySCENIC rerun was performed.
Approx. Recovery Index is treated as an approximate state-shift-derived metric, not a formal geometric projection model.

Metric-selection notes:
    Checked Table3 integrated priority table(s), but they did not contain CellOracle shift/Approx. RI metric columns; selected celloracle_run/ko_round1/round1_ko_master_table.csv instead.
