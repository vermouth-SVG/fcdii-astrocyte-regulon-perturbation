Supplementary Fig. S3. Complete CellOracle in silico perturbation metrics and randomized-control assessment.

(A) Column-scaled heatmap of CellOracle round1 perturbation metrics for NFE2L2, THRB, BHLHE40, and SOX2. Metrics include mean shift, Approx. Recovery Index, net shift, group-specific shift, and direction agreement. (B) Quantitative perturbation summary showing mean shift length versus Approx. Recovery Index. NFE2L2 showed the highest mean shift, whereas THRB showed the highest Approx. Recovery Index. (C-F) Representative simulated in silico KO vector fields and randomized controls for the four candidate TFs. The Approx. Recovery Index is a state-shift-derived summary metric and should not be interpreted as experimental-knockout validation.
